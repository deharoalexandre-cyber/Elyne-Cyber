# Elyne Cyber

**English · [Français](README.md)**

Elyne Cyber combines **local defense** with adversarial training editions. The defense service observes system signals, evaluates them against its policy, and applies bounded responses. Models may contribute to analysis; action authorization remains deterministic.

| Edition | Role | Website positioning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elyne Cyber** | Local observation, decision, and response. | Production |
| **Cyber Red** | Real attacks in an authorized laboratory to train SOC and CERT teams. | Training |
| **Cyber Hybrid** | An adversarial edition combining offensive and defensive behavior. | Training |

Elyne Cyber runs as a service separate from **EAS Defense**. Tests have shown functional independence while EAS was stopped and, later, narrowly scoped Windows denials of access to EAS secrets. These observations do not prove that compromise of the host or Cyber is impossible.
